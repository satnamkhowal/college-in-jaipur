# Legacy content migration

Started: 24 September 2026

## Objective

Use the legacy education-portal mirror as a topic-discovery and coverage reference while building original, Jaipur-focused pages for College in Jaipur.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Do not copy or reuse third-party logos, banners, screenshots, branded illustrations or promotional graphics.
- Do not hotlink assets from the legacy mirror.
- Do not publish copied promotional paragraphs. Rewrite content for the College in Jaipur audience and local intent.
- Treat fees, rankings, college counts, admission dates, approvals, seats, cutoffs and placements as mutable facts. Verify them from official institution or regulator sources before publication.
- Prefer owned, licensed, institution-provided or otherwise permitted visuals.
- Keep migration changes on a review branch until checked.

## What was intentionally excluded

The source mirror contains many third-party asset folders and hundreds of brand references. None of those graphics or visual assets are part of this migration batch. Numeric claims from the mirror were also not copied into the live target content because they are time-sensitive and require primary-source verification.
